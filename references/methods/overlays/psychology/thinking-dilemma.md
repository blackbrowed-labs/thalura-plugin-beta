# Psychology — Thinking & Dilemma Methods

## Thought Experiments (Gedankenexperimente)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Exploring psychological phenomena through hypothetical scenarios — e.g., split-brain thought experiments, nature vs. nurture scenarios, ethical dilemmas in research design. Connects to the Bildungsplan emphasis on examining assumptions about human behavior.

---

## Dilemma Discussion (Dilemma-Diskussion)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Ethical dilemmas in psychology are a core topic: confidentiality vs. duty to warn, informed consent in research, balancing individual rights with scientific knowledge. Kohlberg's moral development stages can be both content and analytical tool.

---

## Opinion Line (Positionslinie / Meinungslinie)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Positioning on psychological controversies — nature vs. nurture, free will vs. determinism, medication vs. therapy, normality vs. abnormality. Makes implicit assumptions about human behavior visible and discussable.

---

## Conceptual Analysis (Begriffsanalyse)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Defining and refining core psychological concepts — intelligence, personality, consciousness, normality, mental health. Testing definitions against empirical cases and counterexamples mirrors the operationalization process in psychological research.
