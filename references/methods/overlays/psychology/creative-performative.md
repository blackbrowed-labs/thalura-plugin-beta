# Psychology — Creative & Performative Methods

## Standbild / Freeze Frame (Tableau Vivant)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Representing psychological dynamics — e.g., group conformity, power relationships, attachment styles — through physical arrangement. Useful for embodying abstract interpersonal concepts.

---

## Role Play & Simulation (Rollenspiel)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Central to Psychology teaching: simulating therapeutic conversations, counseling scenarios, social psychology experiments (e.g., conformity, obedience), and interpersonal conflict resolution. Debriefing phase is essential for psychological reflection.

---

## Hot Seat (Heißer Stuhl)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Student in character as a psychologist from a specific paradigm, a research subject, or a historical figure in psychology (Freud, Milgram, Bandura). Audience questions deepen understanding of theoretical positions.

---

## Creative Writing

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Writing case study narratives, psychological profiles, or reflective journal entries from different perspectives. Supports the Bildungsplan's emphasis on Selbstreflexion.

---

## Digital Storytelling

**Rating:** low

**Notes:** Presenting psychological research findings or biographical portraits of influential psychologists. Time-intensive relative to psychology-specific learning outcomes.
