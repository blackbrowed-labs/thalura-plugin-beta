# Psychology — Activation & Warm-Up Methods

## Brainstorming / Clustering

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Activating prior conceptions (Vorwissen) about psychological phenomena; surfacing everyday psychological theories (Alltagstheorien) before introducing scientific perspectives.

---

## Buzz Groups / Murmelgruppe

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Quick exchange on case examples (Fallbeispiele) or personal observations of psychological phenomena before plenary discussion.

---

## Blitzlicht (Lightning Round)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Self-reflection activator — students share initial reactions to psychological scenarios or introspective prompts, building awareness of their own cognitive and emotional processes.

---

## Gamification (Spielerische Elemente)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Useful for reviewing psychological concepts, paradigms, or key researchers. Quiz formats work well for terminology and classification tasks.
