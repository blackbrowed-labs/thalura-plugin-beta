# Psychology — Cooperative Learning Methods

## Think-Pair-Share

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Core method for processing case studies (Fallbeispiele) and connecting psychological theory to personal experience. Explicitly supported by the Bildungsplan's emphasis on cooperative learning.

---

## Placemat (Placemat-Methode)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Excellent for multi-perspective analysis — e.g., each student applies a different psychological paradigm (psychoanalytic, behaviorist, cognitive, humanistic) to the same phenomenon.

---

## Kugellager (Inside-Outside Circle / Ball Bearing)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Quick comparison of different interpretations of psychological phenomena or case analyses.

---

## Jigsaw (Gruppenpuzzle / Expertengruppen)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Ideal for paradigm comparison (Paradigmenvergleich) — expert groups study one psychological school of thought, then teach it in home groups. Directly supports the Bildungsplan competency of comparing paradigms.

---

## Gallery Walk (Museumsgang / Galerierundgang)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Presenting research designs, case analyses, or paradigm comparisons as visual products.

---

## Snowballing (Schneeball-Methode)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Progressive refinement of psychological hypotheses or explanations for observed behavior.
