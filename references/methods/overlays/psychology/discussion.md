# Psychology — Discussion & Dialogue Methods

## Socratic Dialogue (Sokratisches Gespräch)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Useful for exploring foundational psychological questions (e.g., "What determines behavior?", "Can personality change?"). Less central than in Philosophy but valuable for examining assumptions about human nature.

---

## Fishbowl Discussion (Fishbowl-Diskussion)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Structured discussion of controversial psychological topics — nature vs. nurture, ethics of psychological research, diagnostic labeling, therapeutic approaches. Outer circle practices behavioral observation skills.

---

## Pro-Contra Debate (Pro-Kontra-Debatte / Podiumsdiskussion)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Debating psychological controversies: free will vs. determinism, medication vs. therapy, ethical boundaries of experiments, nature vs. nurture. Paradigm representatives can argue from different psychological schools.

---

## Philosophical Conversation (Philosophisches Gespräch)

**Rating:** low

**Notes:** Psychology emphasizes empirical evidence over open philosophical inquiry. Useful occasionally for foundational questions about consciousness or the mind-body problem, but not a core psychology method.
