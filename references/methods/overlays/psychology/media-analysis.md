# Psychology — Media & Analysis Methods

## Film / Video Analysis (Filmanalyse)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Analyzing psychological phenomena in film clips — attachment behavior, group dynamics, mental health portrayals, therapeutic processes. Documentary footage of classic experiments (Milgram, Zimbardo, Harlow) is a staple of psychology teaching.

---

## Image / Cartoon Analysis (Bild- / Karikaturanalyse)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Analyzing visual representations of psychological concepts, optical illusions for perception units, or media portrayals of mental health. Cartoons about therapy or psychological stereotypes can serve as discussion starters.
