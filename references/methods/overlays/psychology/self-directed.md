# Psychology — Self-Directed & Station-Based Learning

## Station Learning (Stationenlernen / Lerntheke)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Stations can feature different psychological paradigms, mini-experiments, case studies, and self-assessment instruments. Works well for introductory units covering multiple perspectives.

---

## WebQuest

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Researching psychological studies, biographical profiles of psychologists, or current debates in psychology from curated sources. Supports critical evaluation of online psychological claims.

---

## Project-Based Learning (Projektarbeit)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Designing and conducting small-scale psychological investigations — surveys, observational studies, simple experiments. Directly supports the Bildungsplan competency of understanding empirical research methods (empirische Forschungsmethoden).

---

## Task-Based Learning / TBL

**Rating:** none

**Notes:** Not applicable. English language teaching methodology.
