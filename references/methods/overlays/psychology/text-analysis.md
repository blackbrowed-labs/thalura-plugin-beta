# Psychology — Text-Based Methods

## Close Reading (Textanalyse)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Analyzing primary psychological texts — research abstracts, case study reports, excerpts from foundational works (Freud, Piaget, Bandura). Focus on identifying hypotheses, methods, findings, and theoretical framing.

---

## Reading Log (Lesetagebuch)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Tracking reactions and reflections while reading longer psychological texts or case study collections. Supports the self-reflection (Selbstreflexion) emphasis in the Bildungsplan.

---

## Literature Circles

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Adaptable with psychology-specific roles: Research Analyst (methods and findings), Paradigm Identifier (theoretical framework), Real-World Connector (everyday applications), Critical Evaluator (limitations and ethics).

---

## Argument Reconstruction (Argumentrekonstruktion)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Reconstructing the reasoning in psychological theories and research conclusions. Identifying premises, evidence, and logical gaps in competing explanations of behavior. Essential for paradigm comparison.

---

## Hermeneutical Circle (Hermeneutischer Zirkel)

**Rating:** low

**Notes:** Psychology relies on empirical methods rather than hermeneutical text interpretation. May have limited use for deep reading of qualitative research or psychoanalytic texts, but not a standard approach.
