# Psychology — Writing & Reflection Methods

## Process Writing / Scaffolded Essay Writing

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Structuring psychological essays — especially paradigm comparison essays and case-based argumentation. The scaffolded approach supports building the specific text structure required for psychology assessments (Klausuren).

---

## Concept Mapping / Mind Mapping

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Visualizing relationships between psychological concepts, paradigms, and their key constructs. Excellent for mapping the connections between theories, researchers, and empirical findings within a topic area.

---

## Philosophical Essay (Erörterung / Philosophischer Essay)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** The argumentative structure transfers well to psychology essays, but psychology essays require empirical evidence and paradigm-based reasoning rather than purely philosophical argumentation. Useful as a structural model.

---

## Schreibgespräch (Silent Written Conversation)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Silent written exchange about case studies, psychological phenomena, or research findings. Reduces social pressure and encourages reflective processing — aligning with the Bildungsplan emphasis on Selbstreflexion and thoughtful engagement with psychological content.
