# Religion — Media & Analysis Methods

## Film / Video Analysis (Filmanalyse)

**Rating:** moderate

**Notes:** Religious films, documentaries on faith communities, and ethical scenario clips are used but not central to the curriculum. Effective for exploring theodicy narratives, interreligious encounters, or social justice themes in film. Requires careful framing to connect visual narratives to theological analysis (theologische Analyse).

---

## Image / Cartoon Analysis (Bild- / Karikaturanalyse)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Religious art (religiöse Kunst), iconography (Ikonographie), and sacred symbols are core material in Religion instruction. Analyzing medieval paintings, religious architecture, or editorial cartoons on religion-in-society topics develops interpretive competency (Deutungskompetenz). Students learn to read visual theological language.

---

## Sprachmittlung / Mediation

**Rating:** none

**Notes:** English-specific methodology with no direct application in Religion instruction. Cross-linguistic mediation tasks are part of the foreign language curriculum, not the Religion curriculum.
