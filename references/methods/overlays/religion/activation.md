# Religion — Activation & Warm-Up Methods

## Brainstorming / Clustering

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Activating prior knowledge on theological concepts (theologische Begriffe), biblical narratives, or ethical dilemmas (ethische Dilemmata). Clustering works well for mapping connections between religious traditions, key figures, and doctrinal concepts.

---

## Buzz Groups / Murmelgruppe

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Ideal for processing after reading biblical or theological source texts (theologische Quellentexte). Encourages personal reflection (Selbstreflexion) before plenary discussion, lowering the threshold for sharing on sensitive faith topics.

---

## Blitzlicht (Lightning Round)

**Rating:** high

**Notes:** Quick personal positioning on faith and ethics questions — e.g., "What does justice (Gerechtigkeit) mean to you?" or "How do you relate to this biblical passage?" Makes diverse perspectives visible, supporting dialogue competency (Dialogkompetenz).

---

## Gamification (Spielerische Elemente)

**Rating:** low

**Notes:** Limited application — theological depth (theologische Tiefe) is hard to achieve through gamification. Occasional use for factual recall (e.g., world religion quiz, liturgical calendar) but not suited for the reflective and dialogical core of Religion instruction.
