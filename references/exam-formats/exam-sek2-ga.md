# Sek II Exam — Standard Level (Klausur Sek II, grundlegendes Anforderungsniveau)

A formal written exam at standard level (grundlegendes Anforderungsniveau / gA) in the Studienstufe (semesters S1–S4). Klausuren at gA level test competencies with a focus on AB II, using the operators prescribed for the current Abiturjahrgang.

---

## Metadata

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Applicable subjects | E, P, Psy, R |
| Applicable school types | Gymnasium, Stadtteilschule |
| Grade range | S1–S4 |
| Course level (Anforderungsniveau) | gA (grundlegendes Anforderungsniveau) |
| Typical duration | 90 min (standard); S3–S4 under Abiturbedingungen: per APO-AH §24 |

## Structure and Sections

A gA Klausur in the Studienstufe follows a formal structure with stimulus material, sequenced tasks using prescribed operators, and a mandatory Erwartungshorizont (grading rubric). In S3–S4, Klausuren in written exam subjects (schriftliche Prüfungsfächer) are written under Abiturbedingungen, matching the scope and format of the A-Heft regulations.

### General Structure
1. **Stimulus material** — text(s), source document(s), visual material, or audio
2. **Task sequence** — typically 3 tasks with increasing complexity (AB I → AB II → AB III)
3. **Task choice** — subject-dependent (see Subject-Specific Notes)

### Duration Rules

| Semester | Duration | Regulatory Basis |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| S1–S2 | min 90 min | BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9 |
| S3–S4 (exam subjects) | 4 Zeitstunden (240 min) | APO-AH §24, p. 20 |
| S3–S4 (non-exam subjects) | min 90 min | BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9 |

### Frequency Rules

| Teaching Hours | Klausuren per Year | Regulatory Basis |
|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 2–3 hours/week + Seminar | min 1 per Semester | BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9 |
| 5 hours/week (incl. Seminar) | min 3 per year | BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9 |
| 6 hours/week (incl. Seminar) | min 4 per year | BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9 |

One Klausur per Semester is replaced by the mandatory Präsentationsleistung (in the subject chosen by the student at start of S1 and S3). Beyond this, max one additional Klausur per year per subject may be replaced by an entsprechende Leistung. Klausuren unter Abiturbedingungen cannot be replaced (BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b, pp. 9–10).

## Operator Requirements per Performance Level (Anforderungsbereich)

Operators are those prescribed for the current Abiturjahrgang via the A-Heft. Focus (Schwerpunkt) on AB II.

| AB Level | Target Weight | Description |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| AB I | ~30% | Reproduktion — reproduce, describe, identify |
| AB II | ~40% | Reorganisation/Transfer — analyze, explain, compare, apply |
| AB III | ~30% | Reflexion/Bewertung — evaluate, discuss, assess, create |

Per BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3a, p. 7: Klausuren "umfassen alle Verständnisebenen von der Reproduktion bis zur Problemlösung und verlangen auch Transferleistungen." Focus on AB II per Abiturrichtlinie, Section 3, p. 6.

## Number and Type of Tasks

- Typically 3 main tasks (with possible subtasks)
- Tasks use prescribed operators and follow AB progression
- Task choice depends on subject (see Subject-Specific Notes below)

| Duden (German) | Subject-dependent | For Philosophy/Religion |

## Korrektur und Bewertung (Correction and Grading)

- Erwartungshorizont (grading rubric) is mandatory
- "Ausreichend" (5 Punkte) requires at least 50% of expected performance (BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b, p. 10)
- Content and language errors both factor into the grade; in all subjects, errors in linguistic accuracy, expressiveness, and structured argumentation must be considered (BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3a, p. 7)
- Return within 3 weeks (BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3a, p. 7)
- If more than 1/3 of students score mangelhaft or below, Klassenleitung and Schulleitung must be informed (BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3a, pp. 7–8)

## Subject-Specific Notes

### English

- **Klausur structure** per ARL English: Schreibaufgabe (writing task) + optional Sprachmittlung (mediation) and/or Hörverstehen (listening comprehension)
- **Sprachmittlung** may appear in S1–S2 Klausuren (ARL English, Section 4.2.2, pp. 12–13): accounts for ~25% of the total score when included
- **Hörverstehen** may appear as a component: accounts for ~20% when included (ARL English, Section 4.2.3, p. 13)
- **Content:Language weighting** = 40:60 — language performance is weighted more heavily than content (ARL English, Section 4.4.2, pp. 14–16)
- **GER target level**: B2 for gA (ARL English, Section 2, pp. 4–5)
- **Word limits for Schreibaufgabe**: max ~800 words for gA (ARL English, Section 4.2.1, pp. 11–12)
- **AB distribution for Schreibaufgabe**: Comprehension ~30%, Analysis ~40%, Comment/Creative ~30% (ARL English, Section 4.2.1, pp. 11–12)
- **Task choice**: 4 tasks presented, student works on 3 (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5) — applies to Abiturbedingungen Klausuren

### Philosophy

- **Aufgabenarten** (task types) per ARL Philosophy: discursive-argumentative tasks, tasks on philosophical statements, tasks on presentational materials (ARL Philosophy, pp. 5–7)
- **Task choice**: 3 tasks presented, student chooses 1 (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5) — applies to Abiturbedingungen Klausuren
- **Competencies assessed**: Wahrnehmungs-/Deutungskompetenz, Argumentations-/Urteilskompetenz, Darstellungskompetenz
- **Reflexionskompetenz** is the guiding competency — the ability to critically examine philosophical positions (ARL Philosophy, p. 4)
- Exam written in German

### Psychology

- **Aufgabenart**: Materialgebundene Aufgaben (material-based tasks) using fachbezogene Texte, Untersuchungsergebnisse, Fallbeispiele, Grafiken, or Tabellen (ARL Psychology, Anlage 23)
- **Task choice**: 3 tasks presented, student chooses 1 (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5) — applies to Abiturbedingungen Klausuren
- **Competencies assessed**: Analyse-, Urteils- und Handlungskompetenz
- Tasks must reference at least 2 psychologische Hauptstroemungen (paradigms): tiefenpsychologisch + behavioristisch minimum for gA
- Tasks centrally set by BSB ("zentral gestellt")
- **Hilfsmittel**: specified per exam task (no standing list)
- No explicit content:language weighting — content is primary; up to 2 Notenpunkte deduction for severe language deficiencies
- Exam written in German

### Religion

- **Aufgabenarten** (task types) per ARL Religion: Textaufgabe (text-based task), Erweiterte Textaufgabe (extended text task), Gestaltungsaufgabe (creative/design task) (ARL Religion, pp. 5–7)
- **Task choice**: 3 tasks presented, student chooses 1 (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5) — applies to Abiturbedingungen Klausuren
- **Competencies assessed**: Sachkompetenz, Deutungskompetenz, Urteilskompetenz, Dialogkompetenz
- Exam written in German
