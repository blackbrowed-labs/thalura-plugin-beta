# Sek II Exam — Advanced Level (Klausur Sek II, erhöhtes Anforderungsniveau)

A formal written exam at advanced level (erhöhtes Anforderungsniveau / eA) in the Studienstufe (semesters S1–S4). Klausuren at eA level demand higher complexity and a greater proportion of AB III tasks compared to gA. In S3–S4, these Klausuren are written under Abiturbedingungen with extended duration.

---

| Field | Value |

| Applicable subjects | E, P, Psy, R |
| Applicable school types | Gymnasium, Stadtteilschule |

## Structure and Sections

An eA Klausur follows the same formal structure as gA but with higher complexity expectations: longer and more demanding texts, more complex tasks, and a greater proportion of AB III. In S3–S4, Klausuren in written exam subjects are written under Abiturbedingungen, matching the scope and format of the A-Heft regulations.

### General Structure
1. **Stimulus material** — text(s), source document(s), visual material, or audio (typically longer/more complex than gA)
2. **Task sequence** — typically 3 tasks with increasing complexity (AB I → AB II → AB III)
3. **Task choice** — subject-dependent (see Subject-Specific Notes)

### Duration Rules

| Semester | Duration | Regulatory Basis |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| S1–S2 | min 90 min | BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9 |
| S3–S4 (exam subjects) | 5 Zeitstunden (300 min) | APO-AH §24, p. 20 |
| S3–S4 (non-exam subjects) | min 90 min | BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9 |

Note: eA subjects receive 5 Zeitstunden (vs. 4 for all other subjects including gA), per APO-AH §24, p. 20.

### Frequency Rules

Same as gA — see `exam-sek2-ga.md` for details. eA subjects are typically taught 4–6 hours/week, resulting in 3–4 Klausuren per year.

## Operator Requirements per Performance Level (Anforderungsbereich)

Same operator set as gA (prescribed by the A-Heft for the current Abiturjahrgang), but with higher expectations for depth and complexity, particularly at AB III.

| AB Level | Target Weight | Description |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| AB I | ~25% | Reproduktion — reproduce, describe, identify |
| AB II | ~40% | Reorganisation/Transfer — analyze, explain, compare, apply |
| AB III | ~35% | Reflexion/Bewertung — evaluate, discuss, assess, create |

The higher AB III proportion reflects the erhöhtes Anforderungsniveau: deeper analysis, more independent judgment, and more sophisticated argumentation are expected. Focus remains on AB II per Abiturrichtlinie, Section 3, p. 6.

## Number and Type of Tasks

- Typically 3 main tasks (with possible subtasks)
- Tasks use prescribed operators and follow AB progression
- Task choice depends on subject (see Subject-Specific Notes)
- Tasks are more open-ended and demanding than gA equivalents

## Permitted Aids (Hilfsmittel)

| Aid | Permitted | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Operator list | Yes | From the applicable A-Heft |
| Monolingual dictionary (English) | Yes | Standard for English eA |
| Bilingual dictionary (English) | No | Not permitted for eA |
| Duden (German) | Subject-dependent | For Philosophy/Religion |

## Korrektur und Bewertung (Correction and Grading)

Same rules as gA — see `exam-sek2-ga.md`. Key points:
- Erwartungshorizont mandatory
- "Ausreichend" (5 Punkte) = min 50% of expected performance
- Content and language both assessed; language errors count in all subjects
- Return within 3 weeks

## Subject-Specific Notes

### English

- **Klausur structure** per ARL English: Schreibaufgabe (writing task) + optional Sprachmittlung (mediation) and/or Hörverstehen (listening comprehension)
- **Sprachmittlung** may appear in S1–S2 Klausuren (ARL English, Section 4.2.2, pp. 12–13): ~25% when included
- **Hörverstehen** may appear: ~20% when included (ARL English, Section 4.2.3, p. 13)
- **Content:Language weighting** = 40:60 (ARL English, Section 4.4.2, pp. 14–16)
- **GER target level**: B2+ for eA (ARL English, Section 2, pp. 4–5) — higher than gA (B2)
- **Word limits for Schreibaufgabe**: max ~1000 words for eA (ARL English, Section 4.2.1, pp. 11–12)
- **AB distribution for Schreibaufgabe**: Comprehension ~30%, Analysis ~40%, Comment/Creative ~30% (ARL English, Section 4.2.1, pp. 11–12)
- **Task choice**: 4 tasks presented, student works on 3 (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5)
- Texts are longer and linguistically more demanding than gA

### Philosophy

- **Aufgabenarten**: discursive-argumentative tasks, philosophical statement tasks, presentational material tasks (ARL Philosophy, pp. 5–7)
- **Task choice**: 3 tasks presented, student chooses 1 (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5)
- **Competencies**: Wahrnehmungs-/Deutungskompetenz, Argumentations-/Urteilskompetenz, Darstellungskompetenz
- **Reflexionskompetenz** as guiding competency — deeper philosophical engagement expected at eA (ARL Philosophy, p. 4)
- Texts are more complex, arguments must be more differentiated
- Exam written in German

### Psychology

- **Aufgabenart**: Materialgebundene Aufgaben (material-based tasks) using fachbezogene Texte, Untersuchungsergebnisse, Fallbeispiele, Grafiken, or Tabellen (ARL Psychology, Anlage 23)
- **Task choice**: 2 tasks presented, student chooses 1 (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5)
- **Duration**: 330 min (including 30 min Einlese- und Auswahlzeit)
- **Competencies assessed**: Analyse-, Urteils- und Handlungskompetenz
- Tasks must reference at least 3 psychologische Hauptstroemungen (paradigms): tiefenpsychologisch + behavioristisch + kognitivistisch minimum for eA
- 3 task proposals submitted to BSB, from which 2 are selected; tasks must not be limited to one Schulhalbjahr
- No explicit content:language weighting — content is primary; up to 2 Notenpunkte deduction for severe language deficiencies
- More complex paradigm comparison and deeper theoretical engagement expected at eA
- Exam written in German

### Religion

- **Aufgabenarten**: Textaufgabe, Erweiterte Textaufgabe, Gestaltungsaufgabe (ARL Religion, pp. 5–7)
- **Task choice**: 3 tasks presented, student chooses 1 (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5)
- **Competencies**: Sachkompetenz, Deutungskompetenz, Urteilskompetenz, Dialogkompetenz
- More complex theological argumentation and source analysis expected at eA
- Exam written in German
