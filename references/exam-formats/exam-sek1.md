# Sek I Exam (Klausur Sek I / Klassenarbeit)

A formal written exam for secondary level I (grades 5–10) that assesses competencies across all performance levels (Anforderungsbereiche). The first regulated exam format students encounter, with increasing formality from grade 9 onward.

## Structure and Sections

A Sek I exam (Klassenarbeit) is a formal supervised assessment. Tasks are identical for all students in the learning group. The exam covers Reproduktion through Problemlösung/Transfer and must include an Erwartungshorizont (grading rubric).

Typical structure:
1. **Stimulus material** — text, image, source document, or audio (English: listening component possible)
2. **Task sequence** — 2–4 main tasks with increasing complexity (AB I → AB II → AB III)
3. **No formal task choice** in Sek I (all tasks mandatory)

From grade 9 onward, tasks must use the prescribed operators (Operatoren) from the applicable regulations. In grade 10 second half, exams must approximate Studienstufe conditions (min 90 min) to prepare students for the transition.

### Frequency Rules

| Subject Type | Exams per Year | Regulatory Basis |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Foreign languages (E) / Deutsch, from Jg. 9 | 4 Klassenarbeiten | BP Sek I Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9 |
| Other subjects (P, R) | min 2 Klassenarbeiten | BP Sek I Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9 |

One Klassenarbeit per year may be replaced by an "entsprechende Leistung" (equivalent assessment). In foreign languages, a Sprechprüfung (oral exam) replaces one Klassenarbeit once during Sek I (BP Sek I Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9).

## Operator Requirements per Performance Level (Anforderungsbereich)

Operators are introduced from grade 9 onward. The focus (Schwerpunkt) is on AB II.

| AB Level | Target Weight | Description | Example Operators |
|----------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| AB I | ~30% | Reproduktion — recall, identify, describe | name, describe, outline, state |
| AB II | ~40% | Reorganisation/Transfer — analyze, explain, compare | explain, analyze, compare, characterize |
| AB III | ~30% | Reflexion/Problemlösung — evaluate, create, judge | evaluate, discuss, assess, justify |

Focus on AB II per BP Sek I Teil C, Section 3a, p. 7: tasks cover "Reproduktion bis Problemlösung und verlangen auch Transferleistungen."

## Number and Type of Tasks

- Typically 2–4 main tasks, each potentially with subtasks (a, b, c)
- Tasks follow increasing complexity (AB I → II → III)
- All tasks mandatory (no choice)
- Point distribution should reflect AB weighting

### English-Specific Task Types (from grade 9)
- Reading comprehension (Leseverstehen)
- Text production / writing task (Schreibaufgabe)
- Mediation task (Sprachmittlung) — optional, increasingly common from grade 9
- Listening comprehension (Hörverstehen) — may be integrated

## Subject-Specific Notes

### English

- Communicative success and expression weighted higher than formal correctness (BP Sek I Teil C, Section 7g, pp. 21–22; repeated in BP Sek II Teil C, Section 8h, p. 24)
- From grade 10: Klassenarbeiten must include tasks for Textanalyse and gelenkte Textinterpretation (BP Sek I Teil C, Section 7g, p. 22)
- Sprachmittlung (mediation) tasks may appear from grade 9
- All tasks and student responses in English
- Assessment covers: Interkulturelle Kompetenz, Funktionale kommunikative Kompetenz (Sprachrezeption, Sprachproduktion, Interaktion), Sprachlernkompetenz, Sprachbewusstsheit, Text- und Medienkompetenz (BP Sek I Teil C, Section 7g, pp. 21–22)

### Philosophy

- Tasks should test philosophical competencies: Wahrnehmungs-/Deutungskompetenz, Argumentations-/Urteilskompetenz, Darstellungskompetenz
- Stimulus material typically includes philosophical texts, statements, or presentational materials (images, cartoons, diagrams)
- Students must demonstrate ability to reconstruct, analyze, and evaluate philosophical arguments
- Written exams in German

### Psychology

- Psychology at Gymnasium is typically only available as an elective in Jg. 10; Klassenarbeiten assess competencies across all four domains: Fachkompetenz, Methodenkompetenz, Urteilskompetenz, Handlungskompetenz
- **Aufgabenart**: Materialgebundene Aufgaben (material-based tasks) using fachbezogene Texte, Untersuchungsergebnisse, Fallbeispiele, Grafiken, or Tabellen
- Content organized by Lernjahre and modules (M1–M9); tasks must reference at least two psychologische Hauptstroemungen (paradigms)
- Stimulus material typically includes case studies (Fallbeispiele), research findings, or psychological texts
- No explicit content:language weighting — content is primary; up to 2 Notenpunkte deduction for severe language deficiencies
- Written exams in German

### Religion

- Tasks should test religious competencies: Sachkompetenz, Deutungskompetenz, Urteilskompetenz, Dialogkompetenz
- Stimulus material may include biblical texts, theological texts, ethical case studies, or visual sources
- Students must demonstrate ability to describe, interpret, and evaluate religious phenomena and ethical questions
- Written exams in German
