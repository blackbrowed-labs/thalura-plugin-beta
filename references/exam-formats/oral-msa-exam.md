# MSA Oral Exam (Mündliche Prüfung MSA)

An oral exit exam for students acquiring the Mittlerer Schulabschluss (MSA) at grade 10. Stadtteilschule only. The exam format is governed by § 21 Abs. 1b APO-GrundStGy. In addition to the mandatory English central exam, students take an additional oral exam (zusätzliche mündliche Prüfung) in a subject chosen from the school's offering.

## Structure and Sections

The MSA oral exam (zusätzliche mündliche Prüfung im MSA) is a school-level exam where students choose one subject from the school's offering, in addition to the mandatory English exam. The school selects from Pflicht- und Wahlpflichtfächer in the grade-level Stundentafel. Philosophy and Religion are available as Wahlpflichtfächer (Stadtteilschule: Zeilen 15 und 16 der Anlage 4 zur APO-GrundStGy; Musteraufgabe MSA Philosophie, Vorbemerkung, p. 3).

Each Musteraufgabe consists of two parts:

### Teil I — Prüfungsunterlagen (Exam Materials)

1. **Themenbekanntgabe** — Topic announcement, given to the student two weeks before the exam. The topic must relate to content treated in at least 12 lessons of 45 min each (§ 21 Absatz 2 APO-GrundStGy). Holiday periods are excluded per § 108 Satz 1 HmbSG. (Musteraufgabe MSA Philosophie, Vorbemerkung, p. 3)
2. **Prüfungsaufgabe** — The actual exam task, given to the student on exam day at the start of preparation time. No hints about the task may be given before this point.
3. **Lehrkräftematerial** — Teacher materials including:
   - Rahmenplanbezug und unterrichtliche Voraussetzungen (curriculum reference and prerequisites)
   - Impulse / Fragen für das Prüfungsgespräch (prompts for the exam discussion)
   - Erwartungshorizont (grading rubric)
   - Bewertungshinweise (assessment guidelines)
   - Dokumentationsbogen (documentation form)

### Teil II — Erläuterung des Beispiels (Didactic Guidance)

Guidance on topic selection, didactic/methodological considerations, and exam conduct. Intended for teacher orientation, not for direct use as exam material.

### Fachpraktischer Anteil (Practical Component)

For the MSA, the practical component can be included (optional, unlike ESA where it is expected for ESA-level subjects). In gesellschaftswissenschaftliche Fächer (including Philosophy and Religion), practical elements take the form of Rollenspiele or vergleichbare eigenständig durchgeführte sozial-kommunikative Darbietungen (Musteraufgabe MSA Philosophie, Vorbemerkung, p. 3–4). The MSA exam may also take a more traditional oral exam format with higher analytical expectations than ESA.

## Operator Requirements per Performance Level (Anforderungsbereich)

MSA exams should cover Reproduktion through Transfer/Problemlösung. Expectations are higher than ESA, approaching the analytical level expected for transition to Studienstufe. Operators from the Operatoren für gesellschaftswissenschaftliche Fächer are applicable at an age-appropriate level. Grade 10 second half should approximate Studienstufe conditions (BP Sek I Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9).

## Permitted Aids (Hilfsmittel)

Per school and teacher discretion. The Themenbekanntgabe may include preparatory guidance. Students receive the Prüfungsaufgabe at the start of the Vorbereitungszeit on exam day.

## Subject-Specific Notes

### English

- English MSA is a centrally administered exam (zentrale Prüfung), not a school-level oral exam
- No Musteraufgaben provided by BSB for school-level English MSA oral exams
- The BSB Musteraufgaben for MSA cover Philosophy and Religion only

### Philosophy

- Exam conducted in German
- Core competencies: Wahrnehmungs-/Deutungskompetenz, Argumentations-/Urteilskompetenz, Darstellungskompetenz
- Higher analytical expectations than ESA — tasks approach Studienstufe-level philosophical reasoning
- Musteraufgabe includes Rahmenplanbezug to Stadtteilschule Sek I Bildungsplan Philosophy
- Published by: BSB, Referat Unterrichtsentwicklung gesellschaftswissenschaftliche Fächer und Aufgabengebiete, Fachreferentin Dr. Yvonne Lampert, Hamburg 2026
- (Musteraufgabe MSA Philosophie, `stadtteilschule/philosophy/musteraufgaben-msa-philosophy.pdf`)

### Religion

- Exam conducted in German
- Core competencies: Sachkompetenz, Deutungskompetenz, Urteilskompetenz, Dialogkompetenz
- Higher analytical expectations than ESA — tasks approach Studienstufe-level theological reasoning
- Musteraufgabe includes Rahmenplanbezug to Stadtteilschule Sek I Bildungsplan Religion
- Published by: BSB, Referat Unterrichtsentwicklung gesellschaftswissenschaftliche Fächer und Aufgabengebiete, Fachreferent Dr. Jochen Bauer, Hamburg 2026
- (Musteraufgabe MSA Religion, `stadtteilschule/religion/musteraufgaben-msa-religion.pdf`)
