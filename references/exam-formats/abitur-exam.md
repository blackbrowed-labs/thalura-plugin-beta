# Abitur Exam (Abiturklausur)

The centrally regulated written Abitur examination. The most formally governed assessment format, with task structure, content scope, and grading criteria defined by the Abiturrichtlinie (ARL) per subject and the A-Heft for each Abiturjahrgang. Available at both gA and eA levels.

---

## Metadata

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Applicable subjects | E, P, Psy, R |
| Applicable school types | Gymnasium, Stadtteilschule |
| Grade range | S4 (written Abitur examination) |
| Course level (Anforderungsniveau) | gA and eA |
| Typical duration | gA: 4 Zeitstunden (240 min); eA: 5 Zeitstunden (300 min) |

## Structure and Sections

The Abiturklausur is the culminating written examination. Tasks are centrally set by the BSB (Behörde für Schule und Berufsbildung) and must conform to the Abiturrichtlinie per subject and the A-Heft for the specific Abiturjahrgang. The Schwerpunktthemen (focus topics) are defined in the A-Heft.

### General Structure
1. **Stimulus material** — text(s), source document(s), visual/audio material as prescribed
2. **Task sequence** — subject-specific number of tasks (see below)
3. **Task choice** — subject-specific (see Subject-Specific Notes)
4. **Erwartungshorizont** — centrally provided with the exam tasks

### Duration

| Level | Duration | Regulatory Basis |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| gA | 4 Zeitstunden (240 min) | APO-AH §24, p. 20 |
| eA | 5 Zeitstunden (300 min) | APO-AH §24, p. 20 |

### Exam Configuration per APO-AH

- 3 written exams + 1 oral exam per APO-AH §20, p. 18
- At least one exam at eA level
- Subject-specific task counts and choice rules per Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5

## Operator Requirements per Performance Level (Anforderungsbereich)

Operators are those from the A-Heft for the specific Abiturjahrgang. All three AB levels must be represented, with focus on AB II.

| AB Level | Target Weight | Description |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| AB I | ~25–30% | Reproduktion — recall, describe, identify, reproduce |
| AB II | ~40% | Reorganisation/Transfer — analyze, explain, compare, apply |
| AB III | ~30–35% | Reflexion/Bewertung — evaluate, discuss, assess, create |

Per Abiturrichtlinie, Section 3, p. 6: "Die Aufgaben sind in der Regel so zu formulieren, dass der Schwerpunkt der zu erbringenden Leistung im Anforderungsbereich II liegt."

## Number and Type of Tasks

Task count and choice rules per Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5:

| Subject | Tasks Presented | Tasks Worked | Choice Rule |
|---------|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| English | 4 | 3 | Student selects 3 of 4 |
| Philosophy | 3 | 1 | Student selects 1 of 3 |
| Religion | 3 | 1 | Student selects 1 of 3 |

## Permitted Aids (Hilfsmittel)

| Aid | English | Philosophy | Religion |
|-----|---------|-----------|----------|
| Operator list (A-Heft) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Monolingual dictionary | Yes (English) | Yes (Duden) | Yes (Duden) |
| Bilingual dictionary | No | n/a | n/a |

Additional aids as specified in the A-Heft for the specific Abiturjahrgang.

## Korrektur und Bewertung (Correction and Grading)

- **Doppelkorrektur** (double correction) required: Erst- and Zweitkorrektor per Abiturrichtlinie, Section 4, p. 7
- Erwartungshorizont centrally provided
- 15-point scale per APO-AH §9, p. 12
- "Ausreichend" (5 Punkte) = min 50% of expected performance
- For English Hörverstehen component: KMK Bewertungsraster applies (BP Sek II Teil C, Section 7, p. 16)
- Content and language both assessed; language performance weighted per subject ARL

## Subject-Specific Notes

### English

- **Full exam structure** per ARL English:
  - **Schreibaufgabe** (writing task): the core component — text-based, with comprehension, analysis, and comment/creative writing subtasks (ARL English, Section 4.2.1, pp. 11–12)
  - **Sprachmittlung** (mediation): German → English or English → German, ~25% (ARL English, Section 4.2.2, pp. 12–13)
  - **Hörverstehen** (listening comprehension): ~20%, KMK Bewertungsraster applies (ARL English, Section 4.2.3, p. 13)
- **Content:Language = 40:60** (ARL English, Section 4.4.2, pp. 14–16)
- **Word limits**: max ~800 words gA / ~1000 words eA for Schreibaufgabe (ARL English, Section 4.2.1, pp. 11–12)
- **GER level**: B2 (gA) / B2+ (eA) (ARL English, Section 2, pp. 4–5)
- **AB in Schreibaufgabe**: Comprehension ~30% (AB I/II), Analysis ~40% (AB II/III), Comment/Creative ~30% (AB III) (ARL English, Section 4.2.1, pp. 11–12)
- **Schwerpunktthemen**: defined per Abiturjahrgang in the A-Heft
- Entire exam conducted in English

### Philosophy

- **Aufgabenarten** per ARL Philosophy (pp. 5–7):
  1. Discursive-argumentative tasks (erörternd-argumentative Aufgaben)
  2. Tasks on philosophical statements (Aufgaben zu philosophischen Aussagen)
  3. Tasks on presentational materials (Aufgaben zu darstellenden Materialien)
- **3 tasks presented, 1 chosen** (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5)
- **Reflexionskompetenz** as the overarching competency (ARL Philosophy, p. 4)
- **AB distribution**: operator-driven, focus on AB II; AB III requires independent philosophical judgment
- **Schwerpunktthemen**: defined per Abiturjahrgang in the A-Heft
- Exam written in German

### Psychology

- **Aufgabenart**: Materialgebundene Aufgaben (material-based tasks) using fachbezogene Texte, Untersuchungsergebnisse, Fallbeispiele, Grafiken, or Tabellen (ARL Psychology, Anlage 23)
- **gA**: tasks centrally set by BSB ("zentral gestellt"); **eA**: tasks decentrally set — 3 proposals submitted to BSB, from which 2 are selected
- **Task choice**: gA: 3 tasks presented, student chooses 1; eA: 2 tasks presented, student chooses 1 (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5)
- **Competencies assessed**: Analyse-, Urteils- und Handlungskompetenz
- **Paradigm requirements**: gA minimum 2 Hauptstroemungen (tiefenpsychologisch + behavioristisch); eA minimum 3 (tiefenpsychologisch + behavioristisch + kognitivistisch)
- **AB distribution**: AB I ~30%, AB II ~40%, AB III ~30% — operator-driven, focus on AB II
- No explicit content:language weighting — content is primary; up to 2 Notenpunkte deduction for severe language deficiencies
- **Schwerpunktthemen**: defined per Abiturjahrgang in the A-Heft
- Exam written in German

### Religion

- **Aufgabenarten** per ARL Religion (pp. 5–7):
  1. Textaufgabe (text-based task)
  2. Erweiterte Textaufgabe (extended text task with additional materials)
  3. Gestaltungsaufgabe (creative/design task — e.g., sermon, dialogue, letter)
- **3 tasks presented, 1 chosen** (Abiturrichtlinie, Section 2.2, p. 5)
- **Competencies**: Sachkompetenz, Deutungskompetenz, Urteilskompetenz, Dialogkompetenz
- **AB distribution**: operator-driven, focus on AB II
- **Schwerpunktthemen**: defined per Abiturjahrgang in the A-Heft
- Exam written in German
