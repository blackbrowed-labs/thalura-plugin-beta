# Oral Abitur Exam — Presentation Format (Präsentationsprüfung)

The oral Abitur examination in presentation format (Präsentationsprüfung). Consists of two parts: a media-supported presentation followed by an expert discussion (Fachgespräch). The most common oral Abitur format in Hamburg, governed by APO-AH §26 Abs. 3 and the subject-specific Abiturrichtlinie Präsentation.

---

## Metadata

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Applicable subjects | E, P, Psy, R |
| Applicable school types | Gymnasium, Stadtteilschule |
| Grade range | S4 (Abitur oral examination) |
| Course level (Anforderungsniveau) | gA and eA |
| Typical duration | 30 min total (10 min presentation + 20 min Fachgespräch) |

## Structure and Sections

The Präsentationsprüfung has two distinct parts with a fixed time ratio of 1:2.

### Part 1: Presentation (Präsentation) — ~10 min
- Student presents their prepared topic with media support
- Monologic format — examiner does not interrupt
- Must demonstrate independent engagement with the topic
- Assessment: content quality + language/presentation skills

### Part 2: Expert Discussion (Fachgespräch) — ~20 min
- Examiner-led discussion building on the presentation
- Dialogic format — student responds to questions, defends positions, makes connections
- Must cover content areas beyond the presentation (Semesterübergriff)
- Assessment: content quality + interactive language competence

### Timeline

| Event | Timing | Regulatory Basis |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| Task assignment | 2 weeks before exam | Abiturrichtlinie, Section 5.2, pp. 8–9 |
| Documentation delivery | 1 week before exam | Abiturrichtlinie, Section 5.2, pp. 8–9 |
| Exam day | — | APO-AH §26 Abs. 3 |

### Documentation
- Max 2 DIN-A4 pages
- Delivered to the examiner 1 week before the exam
- Contains: topic outline, sources, planned structure
- Does NOT contain the full presentation content

## Operator Requirements per Performance Level (Anforderungsbereich)

All three AB levels must be covered across both parts combined (presentation + Fachgespräch). The Fachgespräch is the primary vehicle for ensuring AB III coverage.

| AB Level | Part 1 (Presentation) | Part 2 (Fachgespräch) |
|----------|----------------------|----------------------|
| AB I | Topic description, context | Factual recall questions |
| AB II | Analysis, explanation, comparison | Analytical follow-up questions |
| AB III | Evaluation, independent judgment | Defense of positions, transfer to new contexts |

### Semesterübergriff Requirement
The exam must cover competencies and content areas from at least 2 semesters. Typically: the student chooses one Inhalts-/Kompetenzbereich, and the examiner supplements with a second area during the Fachgespräch.

## Grading

### Assessment Weight
- The presentation (Part 1) accounts for **max 1/3** of the total grade
- The Fachgespräch (Part 2) accounts for **min 2/3** of the total grade
- Per APO-AH §26 Abs. 5

### Assessment Criteria by Subject

**English** — uses Deskriptorentabellen (descriptor tables) from ARL English, Anlage 2:
- Part 1: content + language (monologic) + presenting skills (3 assessment columns)
- Part 2: content + language (interactive) (2 assessment columns)
- Language performance weighted more heavily than content
- Descriptors for "gut" (11 Punkte) and "ausreichend" (5 Punkte) provided
- Entire exam conducted in English
- (ARL English Präsentation, pp. 4–6)

**Philosophy** — uses Bewertungskriterien:
- Competencies: Wahrnehmungs-/Deutungskompetenz, Argumentations-/Urteilskompetenz, Darstellungskompetenz
- "Gut" = 11 Punkte, "ausreichend" = 05 Punkte (descriptive criteria, not tables)
- (ARL Philosophy Präsentation, pp. 4–6)

**Religion** — uses Bewertungskriterien:
- Competencies: Sachkompetenz, Deutungskompetenz, Urteilskompetenz, Dialogkompetenz
- "Gut" = 11 Punkte, "ausreichend" = 05 Punkte (descriptive criteria, not tables)
- (ARL Religion Präsentation, pp. 4–6)

## Permitted Aids (Hilfsmittel)

- Student may use presentation media (slides, handouts, props)
- No notes or scripts during the presentation itself (free delivery expected)
- During Fachgespräch: no aids
- Specific aid permissions as defined in the subject-specific ARL

## Example Task Skeleton

```
Präsentationsprüfung — [Subject] [gA/eA]
Abitur [Year]

Task (assigned 2 weeks before exam):
[Topic formulation — requires engagement with content from at least
 2 semesters, enabling demonstration of all 3 AB levels]

Example:
"Analyze [specific topic/phenomenon] with reference to [theoretical
 framework / text / concept]. Evaluate [aspect] and present your
 own reasoned position."

Documentation (due 1 week before, max 2 DIN-A4 pages):
- Topic and thesis/guiding question
- Planned structure of presentation
- Key sources and references

Exam structure (30 min total):
  Part 1 — Presentation: ~10 min
  Part 2 — Fachgespräch: ~20 min
    (Examiner extends discussion to second content area
     for Semesterübergriff coverage)
```

## Subject-Specific Notes

### English

- Entire exam conducted in English (presentation and Fachgespräch)
- Assessment uses **Deskriptorentabellen** (descriptor tables) from ARL English 2018, Anlage 2 Englisch — NOT the simpler Bewertungskriterien used by Philosophy/Religion
- **Part 1 assessment columns**: (1) content, (2) language/monologic competence, (3) presenting skills
- **Part 2 assessment columns**: (1) content, (2) language/interactive competence
- Language performance outweighs content performance in the overall assessment
- Student chooses one competency/content area; examiner supplements with a second for Semesterübergriff
- Example eA tasks: analysis of cultural phenomena (film, literature, identity), sociopolitical issues
- Example gA tasks: future-oriented topics, media/technology, social issues
- (ARL English Präsentation, pp. 4–31 — includes 4 example tasks with full descriptors)

### Philosophy

- Exam conducted in German
- Assessment uses **Bewertungskriterien** (qualitative descriptors for gut/ausreichend), not Deskriptorentabellen
- Core competencies assessed: Wahrnehmungs-/Deutungskompetenz, Argumentations-/Urteilskompetenz, Darstellungskompetenz
- Student chooses one content/competency area; examiner supplements for Semesterübergriff
- Tasks often involve philosophical analysis of concepts, thinkers, or applied philosophy topics
- Example eA tasks: epistemological analysis through art (e.g., Magritte), ethical theory application
- Example gA tasks: critique of religion (e.g., Feuerbach), practical philosophy topics
- (ARL Philosophy Präsentation, pp. 4–14 — includes 2 example tasks)

### Psychology

- Exam conducted in German
- Two parts: ~10 min media-supported presentation + ~20 min thematic Fachgespraech
- Duration: ~30 min exam + 30 min preparation time
- Assessment uses **Bewertungskriterien** (qualitative descriptors for gut/ausreichend), not Deskriptorentabellen
- Core competencies assessed: Analyse-, Urteils- und Handlungskompetenz
- Student may propose one Inhalts-/Kompetenzbereich; examiner supplements for Semesteruebergriff
- Exam must cover content from at least 2 semesters (Semesteruebergriff)
- Tasks must reference psychologische Hauptstroemungen (paradigms); paradigm comparison is central
- Presentation max 1/3 of grade; Fachgespraech min 2/3
- Max 2-page A4 documentation submitted 1 week before exam
- Task received 2 weeks before exam

### Religion

- Exam conducted in German
- Assessment uses **Bewertungskriterien** (qualitative descriptors for gut/ausreichend), not Deskriptorentabellen
- Core competencies assessed: Sachkompetenz, Deutungskompetenz, Urteilskompetenz, Dialogkompetenz
- Student chooses one Inhalts-/Kompetenzbereich; examiner supplements for Semesterübergriff
- All 3 AB levels must be represented across both parts
- Tasks may involve theological analysis, ethical case studies, or religious art/symbolism
- Example eA tasks: critique of religion (e.g., Dawkins' "God Delusion"), systematic theology
- Example gA tasks: religious iconography, practical theology topics
- (ARL Religion Präsentation, pp. 4–14 — includes 2 example tasks)
