# Presentation (Referat / Präsentationsleistung)

A student presentation assessment used in both Sek I and Sek II. In the Studienstufe, the mandatory Präsentationsleistung (one per subject choice in S1 and S3) replaces a Klausur and serves as preparation for the oral Abitur Präsentationsprüfung. In Sek I, Referate are part of ongoing classroom assessment.

## Structure and Sections

### Sek I — Referat
A Referat in Sek I is a student-prepared oral presentation on a topic connected to current teaching content. It counts as part of the ongoing classroom assessment (laufende Unterrichtsarbeit).

Structure:
1. **Presentation** — student presents a topic with media support (poster, slides, handout)
2. **Discussion/Q&A** — brief class discussion or teacher questions (optional, at teacher's discretion)
3. **Written component** — optional handout or written summary

### Sek II — Verpflichtende Präsentationsleistung
In the Studienstufe, each student selects a subject at the start of S1 and S3 in which they deliver a mandatory Präsentationsleistung, replacing one Klausur in that semester. This is a formally assessed, individually assigned presentation that is media-supported and includes a Fachgespräch (expert discussion).

Structure:
1. **Presentation** — media-supported individual presentation (not under exam conditions for Sek II presentations)
2. **Fachgespräch** — follow-up discussion with the teacher to verify understanding and depth
3. **Documentation** — written or digital documentation of the presentation content and process

### Key Differences: Referat vs. Präsentationsleistung vs. Präsentationsprüfung

| Aspect | Referat (Sek I) | Präsentationsleistung (Sek II) | Präsentationsprüfung (Abitur) |
|--------|----------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Scope | Ongoing assessment | Replaces one Klausur | Oral Abitur exam |
| Preparation | During class time | Individually, outside class | 2 weeks preparation |
| Supervision | In class | Not under Aufsicht | Exam conditions |
| Fachgespräch | Optional Q&A | Yes | Yes (20 min, 2/3 of grade) |
| Documentation | Optional handout | Required | Max 2 DIN-A4 |
| Regulation | BP Sek I Teil C | BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b | APO-AH §26 Abs. 3 |

## Assessment Criteria

### Sek I — Referat

Assessment covers content, presentation skills, and (where applicable) written documentation:

**Content:**
- Accuracy and relevance of information
- Depth and breadth of treatment
- Logical structure and coherent argumentation
- Use of sources and examples

**Presentation:**
- Clear and engaging delivery
- Appropriate use of media/visual aids
- Time management
- Audience engagement

**Discussion/Interaction:**
- Ability to answer questions
- Defense of positions
- Flexibility in responding to follow-up questions

Per BP Sek I Teil C, Section 4, pp. 11–12 (Referate and Präsentationen listed as legitimate ongoing assessment forms) and Section 6, pp. 14–15 (Bewertungskriterien for oral work).

### Sek II — Präsentationsleistung

Assessment criteria per BP Sek II Teil C, Section 3b, p. 9 and Section 4, pp. 11–12:

**Content:**
- Subject relevance and accuracy
- Depth of analysis and independent engagement
- Connection to current teaching content
- Demonstration of competencies across AB levels

**Presentation and Methodology:**
- Clear structure and argumentation
- Effective use of media
- Free, engaged delivery
- Appropriate academic register

**Fachgespräch:**
- Depth of understanding beyond prepared content
- Ability to make connections and transfer knowledge
- Quality of argumentation in discussion

**Documentation:**
- Written or digital form
- Completeness and accuracy
- Clear presentation of sources

## Subject-Specific Notes

### English

- Sek I Referate and Sek II Präsentationsleistungen are delivered in English (target language)
- Assessment criteria include communicative competence: communicative success weighted higher than formal correctness (BP Sek I Teil C, Section 7g, pp. 21–22; BP Sek II Teil C, Section 8h, p. 24)
- Fachgespräch in English — tests interactive language competence
- Presentation skills: pronunciation, fluency, register, and audience awareness
- For Sek II: preparation for the Abitur Präsentationsprüfung (see `oral-abitur-exam.md`)

### Philosophy

- Presentations in German
- Content should demonstrate philosophical competencies: Wahrnehmungs-/Deutungskompetenz, Argumentations-/Urteilskompetenz, Darstellungskompetenz
- Referate in Sek I: focus on philosophical concepts, thinkers, or ethical dilemmas
- Präsentationsleistung in Sek II: deeper philosophical engagement, independent argumentation expected
- Fachgespräch should test ability to defend philosophical positions and connect to broader philosophical discourse

### Psychology

- Presentations in German
- Content should demonstrate psychological competencies: Fachkompetenz, Methodenkompetenz, Urteilskompetenz, Handlungskompetenz
- Referate in Sek I (Jg. 10 elective): psychological topics with paradigm comparison emphasis; research methodology presentations common
- Praesentationsleistung in Sek II: deeper paradigm analysis, independent evaluation of psychological phenomena using multiple Hauptstroemungen
- Fachgespraech should test ability to apply psychological theories to new cases and defend paradigm-based interpretations

### Religion

- Presentations in German
- Content should demonstrate religious competencies: Sachkompetenz, Deutungskompetenz, Urteilskompetenz, Dialogkompetenz
- Referate in Sek I: focus on religious concepts, texts, ethical questions, or interreligious topics
- Präsentationsleistung in Sek II: deeper theological analysis, independent evaluation of religious and ethical questions
- Fachgespräch should test hermeneutical competence and ability to engage in theological/ethical dialogue
