# Getting started

## The school-year transition

Thalura recognises which school year you are planning for from the conversation —
you do not switch anything. If you say, for example, "Plan a unit for my new
E10a", Thalura recognises that a new school year is meant and creates it.

On first contact with a new school year, Thalura proposes fitting class
continuations along your school type's grade progression: your E9a becomes an
E10a, a grade-10 class continues as S1 in the Oberstufe (the Sek I → Sek II
transition), and a cohort that has finished its Abitur after the final level is
recognised as completed. You can confirm, change, add to, or remove individual
suggestions.

Nothing is created until you confirm, and data from past school years is never
touched.
