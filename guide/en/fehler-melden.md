# When something looks wrong: report a problem

> Only the section below is translated so far. For the rest of this page, see the German version: [`guide/de/fehler-melden.md`](../de/fehler-melden.md).

## Check this first: is Thalura running on your own computer?

Two observations nearly always share one cause: the year overview
(Schuljahresübersicht) is not in your teacher folder, and not a single citation
in a unit plan (Einheitenplanung) is clickable. Both arise when your task in
Claude Cowork runs in the cloud instead of on your own computer. None of it is
reported: no warning appears, no step breaks off, and the documents look
finished — which is why this check comes before all the others.

**How to check:** if the year overview (Schuljahresübersicht) is in your teacher
folder and at least one citation in the curriculum anchoring
(Bildungsplan-Verankerung) is clickable, Thalura worked on your computer.
(Individual citations staying without a link is normal: not every document has an
official online edition.) Otherwise, open **Settings → Cowork**, switch off
**"Run new tasks in the cloud"** — the setting that starts new tasks in the cloud
instead of on this computer — then start a new task and see whether that settles
what you noticed. More on this under [Installation](./installation.md). If it
persists, report the problem as described on this page — that is then exactly the
right route.

This is where things stand today; if that changes, it will be in `CHANGELOG.md`.
